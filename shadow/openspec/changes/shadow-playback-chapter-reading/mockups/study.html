What's new here · 5 items · settings
  1. Pause — the small pill right of Play (phone: over the Play column). K. Freezes the playhead mid-sentence; press again to resume. Play still restarts (its glyph turns ↺ while playing).
  2. Split, reversed — desktop tray “✂ split…” / phone ⋯ → Split…, or X. Stops playback at once; a red line appears at the paused point (or the middle). Drag or tap to move it, hear left / right, Confirm ↵ or Cancel Esc. No line is ever shown before Split.
  3. Audiobook — “▶▶ Listen” beside the chapter name (it reads this chapter), L. Banner + moving playhead, sentence by sentence. Stop: banner button, Space, tap the waveform, or any study action (that press only stops). Then “▶▶ Resume from N+1”. Use “jump to 84” below to see the chapter end.
  4. Phone top — switch to Phone: two lines — ✕ · book · ⋯, then ‹ chapter › · 12 / 87 · ▶▶ Listen. Tap the grabber (or drag it down) for the nav.
  5. Autoplay — “↻ autoplay” in the lane header. With it on, Done & next / next / prev start the new sentence at once (the chip flashes).
