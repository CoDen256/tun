What's new here, and what to click

A · Settings

  • ⚙ is now a screen inside the app, next to Library / Explore / Study / Prepare — not the old settings.html page. Same nav, same look.
  • Single user: no profile picker in the nav or anywhere, no user management.
  • No Save button. Every change saves instantly; a small ✓ saved appears on the row you changed.
  • Theme System / Light / Dark — stored on this device only (it is what the old nav ◐ did; the nav ◐ is gone).
  • Trim silence moved here from Library — still one switch for sentence and recording. Open Library in the nav to see it gone.
  • STT provider: to see the unreachable hint.
  • Books overview is collapsed and read-only — keep it or drop it?

B · Prepare chapters

  • Chapter 2 is open. Hover between two sentences → “⤒ Start new chapter here”. On phone, use ⤒ Show chapter breaks to pin every break point as a tappable pill.
  • Confirm in the page → the chapter divides in two, later chapters renumber, and a toast offers Undo.
  • Each chapter header: ⇣ Merge with next chapter (in-page confirm; the last chapter has none).
  • ✎ Title optional names a chapter inline — accept or reject this one.
  • Edits belong to the active config only. Click Make active on “Tette pauser” to see its own, untouched chapters; Adjust & re-analyse shows the warning that discards them.
